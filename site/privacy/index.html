Privacy

This website doesn't use cookies or tracking.

If you fill in the enquiry form, we receive your email address and anything else you type in. We use it only to reply to your enquiry. We don't add you to a mailing list and we don't sell or share your details.

The form is delivered by Web3Forms, a form-to-email service. Want your enquiry deleted? Reply to any email from us and ask.

The Stamp Card app itself is run by each venue on its own account. Each venue is responsible for the data in its own app.
