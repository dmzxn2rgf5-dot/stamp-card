How it works

No app. No hardware. No training beyond "scan it and tap plus".

  1. Customer scans

    They scan the QR poster on your counter. Their card opens in their phone's browser. No download, no sign-up.

  2. Staff stamp

    Your staff scan the customer's card with their own phone camera and tap +1. The stamp appears on the customer's screen in seconds.

  3. Reward and repeat

    Card full? Staff tap Redeem. Reward given, fresh card. The card stays on their phone for next time.

Why it's free

No subscription

Most digital loyalty apps charge every month, forever. Stamp Card doesn't. You own it.

It runs on Cloudflare's free tier. No server to rent. No database bill. Even a busy venue fits.

Customers don't download anything. Staff don't either. It's all web pages, on phones they already have.

Your brand

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The software is free and open source. It runs on Cloudflare's free tier, so for most venues there's nothing to pay at all. The only optional cost is email card recovery, which needs Cloudflare's paid plan at about $5 a month.

Do my customers need an app?

No. They scan a QR code and their card opens in their phone's browser. It stays there for next time. No download, no account, no password.

Do my staff need an app?

No. Staff scan the customer's card with their normal phone camera and tap how many stamps to give.

What if a customer loses their card?

If you switch on email recovery, customers can save their card to an email address and get it back with a sign-in link.

Can I use my own logo and colours?

Yes. Your name, logo, colours, stamp icon and reward are all set in your browser during setup.

I'm not technical. Can I still use it?

Yes. The guided kit walks you through it step by step, or we can set it up for you.

Does it work outside the UK?

Yes. It works anywhere. It's in English, and there's no currency involved because you're giving stamps, not taking payments.
